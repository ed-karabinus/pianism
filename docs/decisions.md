# Settled decisions

Decisions the owner has made that last beyond one PR, written down so agents stop proposing them again by accident. Read this before proposing a process, tooling or scope change. To change one, say what's new and ask the owner; nothing here is frozen. **Settled** means the owner decided it as a rule; **For now** means it's how things are, and it's open to a better idea. When the owner settles something lasting, add a line in the same PR. Per-requirement product decisions live in the spec and its issues; architecture lives in `docs/adr/`.

| About 2026-10-21 | Settled | Branch protection doesn't require branches to be up to date. Update a branch with `main` only for a real conflict. | Each update reruns the full CI. |

| 2026-11-04 | Settled | A session across midnight shows under each day it has practice in, with that day's minutes; editing or deleting any row changes the one stored session. Reverses ADR 0004's start-day rule, which is amended. | Late-practicing students and sessions of unknown length: today's practice is what was played today. |
| 2026-11-06 | Settled | Weekly consistency (HIS-3) shows under the week's chart: how many of the week's days counted, and how many of the past 52 weeks met the goal. The goal defaults to 3 days a week, and Settings changes it from 1 to 7. A day counts with any row in History, unless Settings sets a minimum, from 10 minutes to 3 hours. Past weeks recount under a changed goal, as no history of goals is kept. The year's line stays away until a week has met the goal. The daily reminder still treats any row as practice. With a daily minimum set, it reads "Practiced 10 minutes or more on 2 of 7 days" (owner, 2026-11-07), matching "Practiced 5 of 7 days". | A calendar year would drop to zero every January, and the spec says nothing resets. A minimum lets someone decide what a good day is: 10 minutes for one person, 2 hours for a conservatory student. |
| 2026-11-05 | Settled | CI runs all of `make mutate` nightly on main, as a report that blocks nothing. | Nothing re-checked older code, and a full run is too long for each PR. |
| 2026-11-06 | Settled | A survivor fails the nightly mutation job, which stays out of the required checks, so it never blocks a merge. | Its first full run had none (232 caught), so any new one is worth acting on. |
| 2026-11-05 | Settled | Muse only reviews: it no longer builds, and takes no slots but the code-complete audit. So the queue (#179) is just the order of work, with no claiming of slots. Each PR's Review section counts its findings that changed code or added a test and that no other review had caught. | Building used up its capacity quickly, and its reviews have caught things the others missed. That count decides whether to keep it, around 2026-12-05. |
| 2026-11-05 | Settled | The welcome (ONB-1) shows once, on the first launch that finds no practice, with a close button and a link reading "Connect a piano". Tips for new users (#49) are decided after it ships. | Nobody gets pushed into a timer, people who already practice never see it, and the link matches the screen it opens. |
| 2026-11-05 | Settled | The daily reminder is off by default, set for 6:00 PM, and says "Time to practice" and "Your piano's waiting." It's queued 14 days ahead and refilled on opening, so someone who stops opening the app stops being reminded. A day with practice, or with a session running, gets no reminder; one paused since an earlier day doesn't count. | The system can't skip a day by itself, so each day's reminder has to be added ahead of time. |
| 2026-11-06 | Settled | Tapping the daily reminder opens Practice, whichever tab was open. It doesn't start a session. | It says "Time to practice", so landing on Settings adds a tap at the moment it's trying to get you started. Found on device check R1. |
| 2026-11-06 | Settled | Anonymous analytics is on by default, with a switch in Settings ("Share anonymous usage"), no prompt and nothing on the welcome. It sends app opens, first launch, each saved session with how it was made, and activation, identified by a hash per install. Any saved session counts toward activation (3 within 7 days of first launch): timer, piano or by hand. Off at first launch, first launch and activation never go, even if it's turned on later. The full list is in ADR 0011. | We can't measure activation without it. The per-install hash lets us count active people, at the cost of Device ID on the App Store label. Counting all sessions fits an app whose purpose is logging practice. Keeping those two signals together keeps the 40% ratio honest. Ed, 2026-11-06. |
| 2026-11-07 | Settled | Pianism won't sell in Europe for now: the EU, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, the UK and Switzerland.
