# The welcome in short sentences

Settled on 2026-11-10, the owner's pick, from a TestFlight tester's note that the welcome read as run-on sentences.

The welcome says:

> Time your practice with one tap. Tag what you worked on. Watch your week build up.
>
> Connect a digital piano to start a session by playing while Pianism is open. Your first 5 are free. After that,
> it's part of Pro.

The piano sentence is the owner's own, with "by playing" added so it doesn't read as needing a piano to start any
session. It replaces the wording settled on 2026-11-07 (the table in [`../decisions.md`](../decisions.md)), and keeps
what that one had to say: playing starts a session only while Pianism is open, and it's Pro after 5 free.

**Why:** one idea per sentence reads faster on a first launch, and "start a session by playing" says plainly what the
piano does.
