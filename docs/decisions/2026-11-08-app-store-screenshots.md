# The App Store screenshots

Settled on 2026-11-08, the owner's picks.

The App Store screenshots have a headline above each on the app's blue, made by `make screenshots` from sample
practice:

- Practice with a piano session running: "Your piano starts the timer"
- History's sessions: "Every session, day by day"
- Time per piece: "See where your time goes"
- Tagging a session: "Tag pieces and skills"
- History's week and goal: "A weekly goal that fits you"

The two showing Pro say so in a smaller line: "First 5 free, then with Pianism Pro" under the piano one, and "With
Pianism Pro" under time per piece.

**Why:** a headline says what each screen is for, and the script re-makes them for any release that changes a screen.
The Pro lines are there because App Review asks a listing to say what needs a purchase (guideline 2.3.2).
