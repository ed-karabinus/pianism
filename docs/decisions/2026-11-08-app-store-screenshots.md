# The App Store screenshots

Settled on 2026-11-08, the owner's picks; the order changed on 2026-11-09, also the owner's pick.

The App Store screenshots have a headline above each on the app's blue, made by `make screenshots` from sample
practice, in this order:

1. History's week and goal: "A weekly goal that fits you"
2. Practice with a piano session running: "Your piano starts the timer"
3. History's sessions: "Every session, day by day"
4. Time per piece: "See where your time goes"
5. Tagging a session: "Tag pieces and skills"

The two showing Pro say so in a smaller line: "First 5 free, then with Pianism Pro" under the piano one, and "With
Pianism Pro" under time per piece.

**Why:** a headline says what each screen is for, and the script re-makes them for any release that changes a screen.
The Pro lines are there because App Review asks a listing to say what needs a purchase (guideline 2.3.2). The week
leads because it works with any piano, as the subtitle, "Time it with a tap or a piano", says: a first screenshot
about the piano starting the timer could tell someone with an acoustic piano that the app isn't for them, and it shows
a Pro feature.
