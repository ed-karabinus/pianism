# The widget says "Ended" once a session reaches the cap, and offers Start

Settled on 2026-11-13 by the owner (device check W9, then a picker: "Ended + Start", and a fresh day from midnight).
The word "Ended" is the Live Activity's.

When a timer session reaches the cap with the app closed, the widget's title changes from "Practicing" to "Ended",
above the day's total stopped at the cap, and Start shows again. Tapping it saves the capped session, ended at the
cap, then starts a new one, whether the app was suspended or not running. Started that way, the new session
replaces the capped one on Practice, so the app's cap banner, which shows until the next Start, doesn't show; the
widget's "Ended" is the sign it was cut off. From midnight on, if the app still hasn't
run, the widget shows a fresh day: "Today", 0m and Start. The capped session is saved when the app next runs.

**Why:** "Practicing" above a clock that had stopped looked broken next to the activity's "Ended", and the owner
wants the app to look clean. "Ended" gives a visual cue that the session ended (the owner's reason), and Start
saves a trip into the app.
