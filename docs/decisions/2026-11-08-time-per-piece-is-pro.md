Settled on 2026-11-08, the owner's pick (D7 in `docs/plans/plan-pro.md`).

Without Pro, History shows a locked "Time per piece" row under the week that opens the Pro screen, with no preview of
the numbers. The week's time by piece stays free.

**Why:** nothing free today is taken away, and nothing shows that doesn't work.
