# Time per piece and skill lists top-level tags, with what's inside them rolled in

Settled on 2026-11-09 (D6, the nested-tags plan, #224); the row's wording and the screen's title are the owner's picks
of 2026-11-10.

Time per piece and skill lists only top-level pieces and skills. A parent's numbers include its children's, each
session once, however many of its children the session has. A parent with practiced children ends its section with
a "3 inside" row and a chevron, which opens the same screen for those children, each with its own numbers, titled
with the parent's name ("Scales"). That screen has no Pieces | Skills switch, as the parent fixes the kind, and no
"inside" rows. The count leaves out children with no practice, as the screen lists only what's been practiced. The
children needn't add up to the parent: time logged on the parent alone isn't listed inside it, and a session with two
children counts toward each.

**Why:** the top level stays as short as before nesting, and a parent reads as everything practiced on it. The
children's own numbers are one tap away, in the same layout, rather than crowding the parent's section.
