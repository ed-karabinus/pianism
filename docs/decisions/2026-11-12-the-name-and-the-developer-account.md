# Pianism keeps its name, from an individual developer account

Settled on 2026-11-12, the owner's pick.

The owner searched the USPTO's trademark records and found no US trademark containing "pianism", so the app keeps
the name. It's published from the owner's individual Apple developer account, not a company's, so the App Store
lists the owner's own name as the seller.

**Why:** the spec's open decisions listed a trademark search before the name was final, and the code-complete audit
of the spec asked for it to be settled. A domain wasn't discussed; the website is at ed-karabinus.github.io/pianism
for now.
