# Pianism keeps its name, from an individual developer account

Settled on 2026-11-12, the owner's pick.

The owner searched the USPTO's trademark records and found no wordmark containing "pianism", so the app keeps the
name. It's published from the owner's individual Apple developer account, not an organisation's.

**Why:** the spec's open decisions listed a trademark search before the name was final (spec discrepancy 13 of the
code-complete audit). A domain wasn't part of this: the website stays at ed-karabinus.github.io/pianism.
