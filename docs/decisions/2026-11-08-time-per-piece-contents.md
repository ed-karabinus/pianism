# What time per piece and skill lists

Settled on 2026-11-08, the owner's picks (D8, and the labels on 2026-11-08); skills added on 2026-11-09, also the
owner's pick.

Time per piece and skill opens on pieces, with a Pieces | Skills switch at the top. Each lists only what's been
practiced, archived ones included, each with its time in all and over the last 30 days, and, from sessions a piano
started in those 30 days, "Playing" and "Paused". A session with several pieces or skills counts fully toward each.

Changed on 2026-11-13, the owner's pick: Playing and Paused moved out of the totals' card into one of their own below
it, headed "Started by your piano, last 30 days", with the footer "Playing is part of the totals above; paused time
isn't counted." In the totals' card they read as a split of the total, which they aren't: playing is part of it, and
paused time is in no total. Dropping the split was offered too; the owner kept it.

**Why:** the labels match Practice's "Paused". Playing is all of a piano session but its pauses, so a silence too
short to pause it counts as playing. Skills came in because the first version left them out by scope, not on merit,
and the owner wanted time per scale key: with skills of their own like "Scales: D minor", the switch shows it. One
row in History with a switch keeps the layout, rather than a second locked row.
