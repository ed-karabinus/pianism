# What time per piece lists

Settled on 2026-11-08, the owner's picks (D8, and the labels on 2026-11-08).

Time per piece lists pieces only, archived ones included, each with its time in all and over the last 30 days, and,
from sessions a piano started in those 30 days, "Playing" and "Paused".

**Why:** the labels match Practice's "Paused". Playing is all of a piano session but its pauses, so a silence too
short to pause it counts as playing.
